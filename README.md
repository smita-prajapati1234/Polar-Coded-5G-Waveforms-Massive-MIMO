# Polar-Coded 5G Waveforms in Massive MIMO

## Overview

This repository presents MATLAB-based research and simulation studies on Polar-coded 5G NR waveforms for Massive MIMO wireless communication systems.

The research focuses on the comparative and parametric analysis of modern 5G NR waveforms, including UFMC, F-OFDM, and FBMC, in combination with 5G NR Polar coding and Massive MIMO systems.

The repository brings together research studies associated with multiple publications addressing waveform performance, spectral efficiency, BER, channel coding, and system parameters.

## Research Focus

The research work covers:

- 5G NR Polar Coding
- UFMC
- F-OFDM
- FBMC
- Massive MIMO
- BER Performance
- Spectral Efficiency
- Parametric Analysis
- Convolutional Coding
- Wireless Channel Modeling
- 5G and Beyond-5G Wireless Communication

## Comparative Research Themes

The publications collectively investigate:

- Performance comparison of Polar-coded 5G NR waveforms
- UFMC, F-OFDM, and FBMC waveform characteristics
- BER performance in Massive MIMO systems
- Spectral efficiency analysis
- Effect of system parameters on performance
- Polar versus convolutional coding
- Impact of Massive MIMO configurations
- Optimization and evaluation of 5G waveform parameters

## MATLAB Simulation

MATLAB-based simulation codes and research studies associated with these publications can be organized in individual project folders within this repository.

The repository is intended to document research methodology, simulation studies, comparative analysis, and reproducibility-oriented materials where applicable.
