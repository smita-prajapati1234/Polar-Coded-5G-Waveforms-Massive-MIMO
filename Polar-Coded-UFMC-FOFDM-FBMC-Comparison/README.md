# Comparison of Polar-Coded 5G NR Waveforms in Massive MIMO

## Overview

This project presents a comparative study of **Polar-coded UFMC, F-OFDM, and FBMC** waveforms in a Massive MIMO-based 5G communication system.

The study focuses on **BER and spectral efficiency** performance of the considered waveforms.

## Publication

**Paper:** A Comparison of Polar Coded 5G NR Waveforms—UFMC, F-OFDM, and FBMC in Massive MIMO System

**Authors:** S. Jolania, R. Sindal, and A. Saxena

**Journal:** Journal of Engineering Science and Technology Review, Vol. 18, No. 2, pp. 132–139, 2025

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.25103/jestr.182.17

## Research Areas

- 5G NR Waveforms
- UFMC
- F-OFDM
- FBMC
- Polar Coding
- Massive MIMO
- BER
- Spectral Efficiency
