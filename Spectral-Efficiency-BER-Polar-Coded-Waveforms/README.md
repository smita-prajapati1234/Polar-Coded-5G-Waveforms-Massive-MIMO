# Spectral Efficiency and BER Performance of 5G NR Polar-Coded Waveforms

## Overview

This project presents MATLAB-based analysis of **5G NR Polar-coded waveforms in a Massive MIMO system**.

The study evaluates **BER and spectral efficiency** performance of different 5G NR waveforms under Massive MIMO conditions.

## Publication

**Paper:** On the Spectral Efficiency and BER Performance of the 5G NR Polar-Coded Waveforms in the Massive MIMO System

**Authors:** S. Jolania, R. Sindal, and A. Saxena

**Journal:** Telecommunications and Radio Engineering, Vol. 84, No. 9, 2025

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1615/TelecomRadEng.2025055395

## Research Areas

- 5G NR Waveforms
- Polar Coding
- Massive MIMO
- BER Analysis
- Spectral Efficiency
- 5G/6G Wireless Communication

## MATLAB Code

MATLAB simulation files associated with the published research work are included in this folder.
